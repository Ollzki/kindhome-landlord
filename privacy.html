Privacy Policy

KindHome Landlord · last updated 19 November 2026 (rev. 2)

Who we are

KindHome Landlord is made by KindHome Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. We are the data controller for the details you give us when you create an account. For the details a landlord keeps about their tenants, the landlord is the controller and we process them on the landlord's behalf.

Contact: support@kindhome.co.uk

What we collect

  • Your account: email address and password (stored securely by our hosting provider; we never see your password).
  • What you enter: names, phone numbers and email addresses of you, your tenants and guarantors; property addresses; tenancy terms; rent amounts and payments you record; certificates and documents; repair reports and notes.
  • Photos: pictures of repairs and properties, and copies of ID documents for right-to-rent checks.
  • Notifications: a push token, so we can send the reminders you ask for.

We do not take card or bank payments in the app. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

Why we use it

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, store and sync your records, show tenants their repairs, documents and rent, and send reminders. Our legal basis is the contract with you, and for tenants' details, the landlord's legitimate interest in managing their tenancy and meeting their legal duties.

Where it is kept, and who helps us

We use two service providers, under contracts that let them use your data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores your records and runs sign-in, on servers in London (UK).
  • Expo passes push notifications to Apple for delivery. A notification can include a short summary, such as a repair's title. Expo is based in the United States, so this is a transfer of data outside the UK.

In demo mode nothing leaves your phone.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or close your account; you can delete your account and export all your data from Settings. The Home Office guidance is to keep a copy of a tenant's right-to-rent document for the tenancy and one year after, then destroy it. The app reminds landlords when that year is up so they can delete it; it does not delete it automatically.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The app is for landlords and tenants aged 18 and over.
