Common questions

Can I try it first?
Yes. On the sign-in screen, tap “Try the demo” to explore a sample portfolio. Nothing leaves your phone.

How do my tenants join?
Add the tenant, then share the invite from their tenancy. They sign up free with the code you send.

How do I export or delete my data?
Open Settings. You can export everything, or delete your account and all its records.

Is this legal advice?
No. The app helps you keep records and reminds you of common deadlines; check anything important with a qualified adviser.
